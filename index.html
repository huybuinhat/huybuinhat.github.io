About

I am a researcher at the Institute of Vietnam and World Economy, Vietnam Academy of Social Sciences, where I have worked since October 2021 in the Regional and Local Economics Division. From 2026 I am reading for a Master of Economics at the University of Queensland.

My research turns on three connected questions: how a circular economy can be measured and built in a middle-income country; how the digital economy is financed and governed; and what actually drives growth across Vietnam’s regions and border provinces. Around these sit related strands on climate impacts in coastal economies and on industrial policy for emerging sectors such as semiconductors.

Methodologically I work across quantitative and qualitative approaches: panel-data regression and modified Solow growth models for regional analysis, PESTLE frameworks and survey instruments for local economic studies, and structured comparative case analysis for international-experience research. My working tools are EViews and SPSS, which I use for both panel estimation and survey analysis; Python at a basic level; and R in progress, which I am learning by reproducing my published border-province panel study as a public replication package. Publications are indexed on Google Scholar.
